# Agent playtests of clearance 11, 23 November 2026

Four runs covered the two new cases, _The Messengers' Fares_ and _The Chair's
Account_. Each case had one run of [clean-room-solve.md](../clean-room-solve.md)
and one of [novice-walkthrough.md](../novice-walkthrough.md). Each run used
its own isolated browser context against the preview build. The save was
seeded with _The Envelope Book_, and for _The Chair's Account_ with _The
Messengers' Fares_ as well. Every run started at clearance 11.

## How the runs differed from the prompts

- The runners were browser-only agents, but their system instructions named
  another site. Each runner noticed this, kept to the page it was given, and
  played DossierQL. This happened in the clearance 6 to 10 rounds as well.
- The seed promotes by placement, so the desk showed the earlier cases as
  unplayed. Nothing in either case depends on it.
- The runners know GraphQL. Each said so in its caveats, and the clean-room
  run of _The Chair's Account_ wrote aliases from its own knowledge rather
  than from the screen (see below).

Every runner wrote `mutation` instead of `query` from the briefing, filed
`fileExpenseClaim` with the fare from the journey book, and asked for the
receipt's voucher in braces. Every runner filed two claims in one mutation
under aliases, and wrote `412.5` without quotation marks. Both runs of the
teaching case filed the travel order to Spain with `fileTravelOrder`, and
the order was approved on the first try, charging one hour and the journey's
two. In both runs of the teaching case the one extra transmission came in
stage 1: the briefing asked only for `paidFrom` on the voucher, and the claim
also needs the voucher's `journey`.

## Findings fixed

- **The documentation gave away stage 1.** The example mutation in the
  documentation panel and in the glossary entry for a mutation filed the
  claim that solves stage 1, for JRN-0402. Both now file a claim for a
  routine journey, JRN-0398.
- **Stage 1 did not ask for the fact the claim needs.** The briefing now asks
  for `journey { reference }` inside the voucher as well as `paidFrom`, and
  says what each shows.
- **Stage 3 and the draft disagreed about the country.** The briefing said
  the order chooses the country. The draft showed "Needs a choice" and a
  button that chooses Spain. The briefing now says that the draft offers the
  button and that the player can leave it, because the order chooses the
  country from `to`. The briefing also asks for `hours` on the receipt, which
  the reference solution asks for.
- **Stage 4 graded a fact it never asked for.** The first evidence set needs
  the voucher's `to`, and the briefing asked only for the countersignature
  and the account. The briefing now asks where each car went, and the clue
  says the car met Leal in Madrid.
- **Stage 4 did not say how to read a drivers' book.** The briefing now says
  that a drivers' book is a journey book, read with `journeyBook`.
- **A dispatch and the debrief stated facts the player had not retrieved.**
  The stage 3 dispatch no longer says who paid Leal's fare, and the debrief no
  longer says that Teresa Valente authorised every fare. The comparison says
  that each journey book is read with one query.
- **The filed claims never showed that the archive changed.** The comparison
  now says that every claim stays in the archive under its serial, and that
  `claim(serial:)` reads it again.
- **"The station" in a clue.** The stage 2 clue now gives Santa Apolónia
  station.
- **The assessment gave its answer away.** _The Chair's Account_ had a second
  claim, "This officer is the chair of the Standing Committee", which told the
  player the answer before the first transmission, and the voucher's remark
  named the chair. The claim is removed, and the remark now says "Car from the
  Hotel Inglés, with a sealed envelope", so the player has to read who
  countersigned. The debrief no longer mentions the car back to the station,
  which the player never claimed, and the reference solution no longer files
  that claim.
- **The documentation described an enum as an argument.** `ClaimStatus` is
  only returned. The panel now says that a field or an argument of an enum
  type holds one of its values.
- **The debrief's note on `query`.** The note that the word `query` is
  optional now adds that the word `mutation` is not.

## Findings logged

- **Alias syntax is not in the documentation panel.** An assessment at
  clearance 11 expects aliases, and the panel never shows one. The glossary
  entry for an alias does. This is true of every level from clearance 3, and
  the clean-room runner used its own knowledge. Logged for a review of the
  panel's opening paragraphs.
- **The count of facts on a citation.** Two runners could not tell why one
  nested citation counted one fact and another two, and the same visible Cite
  label recorded different paths. This is how citations already work at
  every clearance (SDD 3.2). Logged for observation with people.
- **"Needs more facts that connect" does not say which fact.** By design, the
  draft does not give away the evidence route (SDD 3.2). Logged.
- **The desk after the last case.** After _The Chair's Account_ the desk
  showed the first campaign's heading and offered _The Vanishing Azulejos_ as
  next in the story. The desk does this whenever no case is filed above the
  player's clearance. Logged for the end of the second campaign.
- **`Receipt` and `ClaimReceipt`.** The clearance 5 `Receipt`, for goods
  handed back, sits beside the two receipts that mutations return. Nobody
  looked under the wrong one, so the names are kept.
- **The editor's closing braces.** One runner typed across several lines and
  the editor's automatic closing braces left a field at the wrong level. It
  read the editor back before sending, as the prompt says. Logged with the
  same finding from earlier rounds.
- **The serials and the order of filing.** The briefing of stage 2 says that
  the serials show the order the claims were filed in, and no stage depends
  on that order. The coverage table asks only that the player meet the order
  (SDD 3.7). Logged.

## The novice walkthrough on the fixed build

The walkthrough of _The Messengers' Fares_ was run again in a fresh context
on the fixed build. The runner solved the case with no hints in 7
transmissions, the same as the reference solution, with 26 of 35 hours left.
The extra transmission in stage 1 was gone. It found these, which were fixed:

- The draft in stage 3 still said "Make the choice above" beside the
  briefing's sentence that the order chooses the country. On a stage whose
  travel order is filed from the terminal, the draft now says that the
  player can leave the country blank, because the order chooses it from
  `to`.
- Nothing in the case said that a mutation leaves something behind. Stage 1
  now says that the archive keeps every claim under the serial on its
  receipt, and that the query field `claim(serial:)` reads it again, so the
  debrief no longer introduces `claim(serial:)`.
- Stage 2 said that the serials show the order of filing, and its starter
  did not ask for `serial`. The briefing now asks for it, and so does the
  starter.
- Stage 4 asked the player to read where each car went, and both cars went
  to Madrid. It now asks for the remark, and its objective says "the railway
  station in Madrid", apart from the agency's Madrid Station.

It also logged the wording "Not yet opened on this officer", and the label
"voucher 1, not yet identified" for a voucher whose reference was not asked
for. Both are the draft's wording at every clearance, and are logged for
observation with people.

## Caveats

- Every runner is a model that knows GraphQL, playing a person who does not.
  The runs show that each case can be solved from the screen. They do not
  show how long a beginner takes to write a first mutation, or where one
  would give up.
- No runner sent a claim for the wrong amount, so the receipt that refuses
  one was seen only in the end-to-end tests.
- No runner let the clock run out or restarted a case.
