## Findings and what was done

### Fixed

1. **Two events in one hour, and only the last was shown** (clean-room and
   novice, _The River Quay_, stage 2). The decisive event was the one
   hidden. The results panel now opens on the first event that arrived,
   and a note says how many more events the watches delivered after it.
2. **A transmission that also passed an event's hour showed the event,
   not the response** (clean-room and novice). The results panel now opens
   on the player's own response, and the note says that an event follows.
3. **A delivered event was labelled with the operation the player had just
   transmitted** (clean-room). An event no longer carries that label.
4. **The response that opened a watch read "The archive returned no data"**
   (clean-room and novice). It now says that the watch is open and no event
   has arrived yet.
5. **The debrief listed each delivered event among "Yours"**, so the list
   disagreed with "You sent N transmissions" (all three runs that finished).
   The list now holds only what the player transmitted.
6. **"Each event costs nothing" read as if keeping watch were free**
   (clean-room). The briefing and the glossary now say that an event costs
   no transmission, and that the player pays the hours Keep watch lets pass.
7. **Nothing said what `harbourLog` returns** (clean-room and novice). The
   stage 3 briefing now says that it returns a list of `HarbourEvent`
   records.
8. **The stage 3 deduction came back although the novice could see the
   matching description** (novice). The response had the signatures' hands
   and not the letter's. The briefing now says that a specimen of
   handwriting is a record, `Hand`, that two things are in the same hand
   when the responses show the same `Hand` on both, and that the old log
   holds a letter signed M whose `hand` to ask for.
9. **The stage 3 briefing read as if it ruled the chair out** (novice). It
   now introduces him as the chair of the Standing Committee, Afonso
   Mascarenhas, and says only that the registry has no specimen of his hand.
10. **The stage 1 objective split "takes … aboard" around a long phrase**
    (novice). It reads "Identify the vessel that took aboard the letter on
    the chair's paper."
11. **Nothing said whether an open watch costs anything** (novice). The
    standing watches list now says that an open watch costs nothing to
    keep, and delivers its events whenever hours pass until it is closed.
12. **The São Brás was bound for Tangier when she berthed at Casablanca**
    (clean-room). Her `boundFor` is now Casablanca, the end of her voyage.
13. **The clock of _The Last Sailing_ named the vessel and the onward port
    before any query** (novice). It now reads "Afonso Mascarenhas is out of
    reach of every warrant in …".
14. **The warrant panel told the player to fill the box `operative` with a
    codename, and showed the officer's identifier** (novice). For an
    officer it now says to fill the box `officer` with the officer's name,
    and gives the name.
15. **`WarrantSubject` said "Fill exactly one of the two boxes"** beside
    three boxes (novice). Its description now covers the officer.
16. **"The officer is in Praia" needed the event's `port`, and nothing said
    so** (novice; the rejected warrant cost 3 hours and the event could not
    be had again). The Praia briefing now says that an event's `port` says
    where it happened, and that the port written in `harbourWatch(port: …)`
    is not part of any response. The schema hint names `HarbourEvent.port`.
17. **The arrival dispatch had the Atlântida berth three hours after she
    sailed on a four-day voyage** (novice). The player now waits four days
    on the quay.
18. **The standing watches were clipped below the editor at 1024 pixels**
    (found before the runs). The terminal's row grows to hold them.
19. **The assessment's "old log" led nowhere** (clean-room, second round).
    The Porto briefing now says that the documentation does not list the
    retired field, and links the glossary entry for a deprecated field.
20. **A document with two operations appeared twice in the debrief, with
    nothing to say which operation each copy ran** (both runs of the
    second round). Each entry, the player's and the reference's, now says
    which operation it transmitted.
21. **The stage 3 sentence about the registry still read as a dead end**
    (novice, second round). It now says that the player cannot compare the
    chair's hand with the registry's, and needs a place where he signed
    something himself.
22. **A transmission for one watch surprised the novice by delivering
    another watch's event** (novice, second round). The stage 2 briefing
    now says that every hour spent, by transmitting or by Keep watch, lets
    every open watch deliver.
23. **The footer's shortcut text was squeezed to one word a line when the
    operation list appeared** (novice, second round). The footer now wraps.

### Logged, with the reason each stands

1. **The results pager numbers delivered events among the transmissions.**
   An event is a response of its own, and the ledger's source links point
   at it by that number. Every event is labelled as delivered by its watch,
   and the debrief counts only what the player transmitted.
2. **A dossier and a lead without a name read "harbour event 2, not yet
   identified"** (novice). That is how every record without a name has read
   since clearance 1 (SDD 3.2), and asking for `reference` names it.
3. **The header's clock figure counts down for a moment after the text has
   changed** (novice). The count is the travel animation of Milestone 15;
   the text a screen reader reads is the true figure (SDD 8.1).
4. **A restart clears the hints taken in the attempt it ends** (novice).
   The completion mode describes the attempt that solved the case (SDD 3.9).
5. **After the last case the assignments desk points at the first unplayed
   case of the course** (novice). The run was seeded by placement, so the
   earlier cases were unplayed. A player who followed the course has none.
6. **The editor's suggestions still offer `harbourLog`.** They offer the
   undocumented fields of clearance 6 too. Recorded in Milestone 16.
7. **The options of a form's box read as disabled in the accessibility
   tree** (three runs). Choosing one works. The state comes from the form
   that clearance 1 has used since 1.0, and needs its own look with a
   screen reader.
8. **A warrant that is accepted goes straight to the debrief, which shows
   no evidence chain** (clean-room, second round). That holds for every
   case that ends in a form, since the debrief of 1.0.
9. **An event missed by a watch cannot be had again in that attempt.** That
   is how a subscription works, and it is why the deprecated log exists. The
   Praia briefing now names the field the claim needs before the watch is
   opened (finding 16).
