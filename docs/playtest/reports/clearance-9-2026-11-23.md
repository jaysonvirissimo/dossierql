# Agent playtests of clearance 9, 23 November 2026

Four runs covered the two new cases, _The Corridor Book_ and _The Cipher
Counter_. Each case had one run of [clean-room-solve.md](../clean-room-solve.md)
and one of [novice-walkthrough.md](../novice-walkthrough.md). Each run used
its own isolated browser context against the preview build. The save was
seeded with _The Door Watch_, and for _The Cipher Counter_ with _The Corridor
Book_ as well. Every run started at clearance 9.

## How the runs differed from the prompts

- The runners were browser-only agents, but their system instructions named
  another site. Each runner noticed this, kept to the page it was given, and
  followed only the game's own links. This happened in the clearance 6, 7
  and 8 rounds as well.
- The seed promotes by placement, so the desk showed the first campaign's
  cases as unplayed. Nothing in either case depends on it.
- A page of 60 entries made the accessibility snapshot too large for two
  runners to reach the Cite buttons, so they sent a narrow page again only to
  cite from it. Their counts below include those transmissions. Two runners
  also could not give the editor focus with a click from their tools, and
  used the keyboard or the fill tool instead. This is logged below and was
  not reproduced by hand.

## Findings fixed

1. **A blanked page loses the entries before the classified one, and nothing
   said so.** Three runs found it. The clerk's note said to start the next
   page after the classified entry, and a player who followed it never saw
   the entries between the page's cursor and that entry: CRC-0050 to
   CRC-0095 in the assessment, COR-0201 in the teaching case. The clerk's
   note now says that those entries did not come back either, and how to
   read them: ask again with the same cursor and a page size that stops
   before the classified entry. The briefing of stage 4 says the same, and
   says why `endCursor` is the wrong cursor on a blanked page.
2. **Nothing said how a page is weighed.** The novice runner of the
   assessment worked it out from the numbers. The description of `entries`
   now says that a page weighs one for each entry and one more for each
   record opened inside an entry.
3. **The debrief of _The Corridor Book_ quoted a cursor the player had not
   seen.** It said the next request starts with `after: "COR-0100"`, which
   only the reference solution's page of 100 produces. It now gives the
   cursor as an example after a page of 100 entries.
4. **The note of PDK-0130 read like a form filled in carelessly**: "made out
   for the holder The Registrar". It now reads "made out in the name of The
   Registrar", in the record, the clue and the evidence.

## Findings logged

- **Stage 1 of _The Corridor Book_ is answered on the first page.** Both
  runners of the case found the answer in the first entry and called it an
  anticlimax. Stage 1 introduces the concept alone, which is the page size
  (SDD 3.7). Cursors are the step of stage 2. A reason to change the page
  size inside stage 1 would be a second step. Logged for the sessions with
  beginners.
- **A page of 60 entries is long to read in Findings.** The runners read it
  in the Raw response tab. A filter in Findings would help any long list,
  not only pages. Logged for observation with people. The briefings let the
  player choose the page size.
- **Citing the pass's `number` bore on no claim.** Stage 2's claim rests on
  the entry's note and its officer, or on a second entry's pass and officer.
  The runner proved the claim from the note and the officer. The draft says
  which facts count, as it does elsewhere.
- **The briefing says to leave the variables panel empty for the first
  page, and the debrief shows the reference sending `{}`.** Both mean that
  the variable has no value. Logged.
- **The objective is the last sentence of the briefing and also stands under
  Objective.** This is the rule of SDD 5.7, rule 5. Not changed.
- **The debrief lists the player's transmissions without numbers**, and the
  Side by side tab is absent when a response holds an error. Both predate
  this milestone. Logged.
- **Two runners could not focus the editor with a click from their
  tools.** Earlier rounds did not report it. Logged for observation with
  people.
- **Register entry CR-0112 and watch entry CRC-0112 look alike.** The book's
  entries are numbered by position, and the clue gives the note in full.
  Logged.

## The novice walkthrough on the fixed build

The novice walkthrough of _The Corridor Book_ was run again, in a fresh
context, on the build with findings 1 to 4 fixed. The runner solved all four
stages with no hints in 10 transmissions (reference 8), with 25 of 35 hours
left. It called the clerk's note in stage 4 the strongest teaching text of
the case, and followed its warning not to start after `endCursor`.

Two of its findings were fixed:

5. **The starter of stage 1 said "Put the argument first: 10".** The runner
   read "first" as "at the front". The comment now says to write
   `(first: 10)` after `entries`, that the argument is called `first`, and
   that 10 is the page size.
6. **Stage 2 gave the weight of 181 without its sum.** The briefing now
   counts it out (60 entries, 60 officers, 60 passes, and the book), says
   that `pageInfo` counts for nothing, and says that opening fewer records
   inside each entry fits more entries on a page. The runner had worked
   that out for itself.

Logged from the rerun: the stage 1 anticlimax again; that the error's path
counts the entries on a page from 0, which no text says; that the
Documentation tab closes its sections when a stage changes; that stage 3
could be solved by editing the cursor in the query instead of using a
variable, which is true of every stage that teaches variables; and that
stage 4 explains how to read the entries before a classified one without
asking the player to do it.

## Caveats

- Every runner already knew cursor pagination from training. The novice
  runners noted that text they found clear may be less clear to a beginner,
  most of all the declaration of `$after: String` and the rule that a null
  travels to the nearest field that may be null.
- No run took a hint or viewed a solution, so the hint ladders of the two
  cases are untested by agents. Validation checks that each is complete.
- The clock was not tuned (SDD 3.3). The hours left are recorded above.
